Here are some useful details to help you plan your cleaning service.

Which areas do you serve?
Spotlight Cleaning Services is based in the Lekki area of Lagos. Contact us with your exact location so we can discuss whether your property is within our service area.
How much does cleaning cost?
The cost depends on the type of service, property size, condition of the space, location and work required. Send us an enquiry so we can discuss your needs and provide a quote.

You can enquire about a one-time deep clean using the form on this page. Share the property type, location and areas you want cleaned so the team can discuss the scope and availability.

Office and workplace cleaning is among the services listed on this website. Contact us to discuss your premises, cleaning requirements and preferred schedule.
